# Northstar Outfitters - Refund Policy

Document Type: Company Policy
Version: 1.0

## Refund Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of the original purchase date.

Items must be unused, in their original condition, and accompanied by proof of purchase.

## Non-Refundable Items

Gift cards and personalized products are not eligible for refunds.

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Customer Support

Customers should contact the support team with their order number to initiate a refund request.
